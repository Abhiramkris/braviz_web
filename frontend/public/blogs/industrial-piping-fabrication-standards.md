# Navigating Industrial Piping Fabrication Standards in KSA

Process piping is the circulatory system of any refinery or manufacturing plant. In Saudi Arabia, the environmental conditions—extreme heat, high humidity in coastal areas, and sand—demand the highest fabrication standards.

## Adherence to ASME Codes
Quality industrial piping requires rigorous adherence to ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers) codes. 

### Best Practices in KSA
- Utilizing advanced orbital welding for critical joints.
- Applying specialized coatings to prevent corrosion.
- Conducting Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) on all high-pressure systems.
